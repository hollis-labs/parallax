The primary Tachyon snapshot is pinned in `tachyon-nav-source.json`: current main 97f0ad6, authored ADR amendment PR78, contract PR77, routing PR79 and host projection PR80, eight manifests and the compiled page registry. Historical planner gaps do not describe that current tree. The fixture retains all fifteen manifest labels/routes and eight groups (the manifest calls Source “Source Control”). Observe Events/Runs/Metrics deliberately aliases Activity/Logs/Metrics, while Services Overview aliases All Services. No new production page is claimed.

AppShell and its public aside/runtime preference model supply the single shell and optional desktop context. The sole narrow drawer is the public OverlaySidebar for modules. The fixture rows reuse OperationsListPage from the landed Operations shell idiom; search/selection/inspection stay local and guarded. Public Base UI Tabs.Root and design TabsList/Trigger/Content supply top tabs and the vertical sub-rail, with addressable hashes and breadcrumbs. Browser back/forward, encoded opaque Work IDs (including literal `board` and separators), whole-path matching and invalid encoding use a pinned excerpt of the landed bounded routing helpers. This is a fixture adapter, not production router or registry-v2 adoption.

The bounded admission example actually processes candidate items and owner hints. Empty successful discovery, denied access, unavailable discovery and degraded discovery are distinct; duplicate ID/route and reserved-route claims are dropped with reasons, missing parents drop their child, orphan groups fall to host-owned More, and explicit group owner wins metadata over an earlier alphabetical claimant. `/dashboard[/...]` and `/plugin-recovery[/...]` remain host-only; `/settings[/...]` belongs only to config-ops; no generic parameter claim is admitted at a host root. Hidden items are removed from navigation without removing their route. Retired session-ops items are disabled with their fixture crash-budget reason and refuse deep links.

Header, hamburger, page-toolbar and row slots consume the same typed local `command`, `navigate` and `modal` contributions. Modal is explicitly a Parallax fixture action; the pinned ADR does not yet allow registry modal intents without an approved widget region. Right-click and deliberate hold target the background row action button. Public useLongPress is background-only and therefore unavailable in the modal navigation drawer; this example does not bypass that contract or claim a modal gesture API.

Scoped shortcuts reuse the Nil registry. Navigation controls use one roving tab stop and arrow keys, menus/Tabs use their public keyboard models, groups expose expanded state, and nested Escape belongs to the child popup. Committed callback leases refuse source/access/layer/root/Activity retirement and use fresh positive controls. Current visible dialog/menu/listbox ownership and exact popup identity are checked at invocation; newer plain foreground focus is preserved. Editable notes may change during composition while shortcut IME/229 branches remain vetoed. Keyboard and touch simulations are synthetic evidence, not OS IME or hardware touch certification.
